Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about PropWise India and AI-assisted agreement analysis

What is PropWise India?

PropWise India is an AI-assisted property intelligence platform designed to help users review property agreements, compare costs, identify potential risks, and better understand real estate-related documents and financial considerations.

Is PropWise India a law firm?

No. PropWise India is a technology platform and does not provide legal, financial, or investment advice.

Users should consult qualified legal or financial professionals before making important property decisions.

How does agreement analysis work?

Uploaded agreements are processed using AI-assisted analysis systems designed to identify common property-related risks, vague clauses, missing protections, and potentially unfavorable terms.

The platform generates informational summaries and downloadable reports to help users review agreements more effectively.

Is AI analysis always accurate?

No. AI-generated analysis may occasionally contain inaccuracies, omissions, or simplified interpretations.

PropWise India does not guarantee legal correctness or completeness of AI-generated outputs.

Are uploaded documents stored permanently?

Uploaded documents and generated reports may be temporarily processed and retained for operational, analytics, debugging, or service improvement purposes.

Please review our Privacy Policy for more details regarding data handling practices.

Who can upload documents?

Users should upload only documents they own or have permission to analyze.

Uploading unauthorized or confidential third-party documents may violate legal or contractual obligations.

What types of agreements can be analyzed?

The platform is primarily designed for Indian property-related agreements such as sale agreements, builder-buyer agreements, allotment letters, and related real estate documents.

Why does the report show risk levels?

Risk levels are generated based on detected clauses, missing protections, ambiguities, and other indicators identified during AI-assisted analysis.

Risk scores are informational indicators only and should not be treated as legal conclusions.

Does PropWise India guarantee safe property investments?

No. PropWise India does not guarantee investment outcomes, legal validity, project safety, builder reliability, or financial performance.

Users should conduct independent due diligence and consult professionals before proceeding with transactions.

Can reports be shared or downloaded?

Yes. Users may download generated reports for personal informational use.

Reports should not be interpreted as legal certification or professional advisory documents.

Does PropWise India sell personal data?

No. PropWise India does not sell personal information to third parties.

How can I contact PropWise India?

You can contact us through the website contact channels for support, questions, or feedback regarding the platform.

Important Disclaimer: PropWise India provides AI-assisted informational tools only and does not replace legal review, financial advice, or independent professional due diligence.
