How to Compare Property Costs in India

Most home buyers compare only the base property price, but the actual ownership cost can be significantly higher. GST, registration charges, maintenance deposits, parking fees, floor rise charges and clubhouse costs can increase the total investment substantially.

PropWise India helps buyers compare real total ownership cost before purchasing apartments, villas or investment properties.

Important Property Charges

  • GST on under-construction properties
  • Stamp duty and registration fees
  • Parking allocation charges
  • Maintenance deposits
  • Clubhouse and amenities charges
  • Floor rise premiums
  • Legal verification fees

Why Property Comparison Matters

Two apartments with similar base prices may have completely different final ownership costs. Comparing complete acquisition expenses helps buyers make financially smarter decisions.
