About PropWise India

AI-Assisted Property Intelligence Platform

PropWise India was built to simplify and improve the way people review property agreements, compare real estate costs, and understand potential risks before making major property decisions.

Real estate agreements are often lengthy, complex, and difficult for everyday buyers to evaluate confidently. Important clauses related to possession timelines, maintenance costs, liabilities, penalties, parking rights, and refund conditions may easily be overlooked.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make property-related information more understandable, accessible, and transparent using technology and AI-assisted analysis tools.

We aim to help users identify potential concerns early and make more informed property decisions.

What PropWise India Provides

  • AI-assisted agreement risk analysis
  • Property comparison tools
  • Cost estimation calculators
  • Risk summaries and downloadable reports
  • Educational and informational property insights

AI Transparency

Certain platform features use artificial intelligence and automated systems to analyze uploaded agreements and generate risk summaries.

AI-generated outputs may occasionally contain inaccuracies, simplified interpretations, or incomplete context. PropWise India does not guarantee legal correctness or completeness of AI-generated analysis.

Users should independently verify important contractual terms and consult qualified legal professionals before making decisions.

Not a Law Firm or Financial Advisor

PropWise India is a technology platform and does not provide legal, financial, tax, investment, or professional advisory services.

Nothing on the platform should be interpreted as legal opinion, legal certification, or investment recommendation.

User Responsibility

Users are responsible for ensuring they have the legal right to upload and analyze documents submitted to the platform.

Final property decisions, legal reviews, negotiations, and due diligence remain the responsibility of the user and their professional advisors.

Our Vision

We believe property decisions should be supported by better transparency, clearer information, and accessible technology.

PropWise India aims to continue improving AI-assisted property intelligence tools while maintaining responsible and transparent usage practices.
