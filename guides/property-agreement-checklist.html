Why This Checklist Matters

Many buyers focus only on property price and location while ignoring agreement clauses that affect long-term ownership.

Small legal clauses can create large financial liabilities later, especially around:

  • Delayed possession
  • Parking disputes
  • Unexpected maintenance charges
  • Area revisions
  • GST and taxation
  • Builder liability exclusions

Most Common Agreement Mistakes

1. Ignoring Delay Compensation

Some builders impose heavy penalties on buyers for late payments but offer very little compensation if the project is delayed.

2. Not Reviewing Maintenance Clauses

Buyers often underestimate recurring maintenance costs, clubhouse fees, and corpus charges.

3. Unclear Parking Rights

Parking allocation and ownership disputes are common in apartment projects.

Important

Never rely solely on builder brochures or verbal commitments. Only clauses written in the agreement are legally enforceable.

Should You Get Legal Review?

Yes. Even reputed builders may include one-sided clauses. A professional review can identify:

  • Missing protections
  • Hidden liabilities
  • Builder-favored conditions
  • Financial exposure risks
