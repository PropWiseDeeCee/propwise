RERA 2.0 FAQ: Everything Indian Homebuyers Need to Know

RERA transformed Indian real estate by improving transparency and buyer protection. RERA 2.0 represents the next phase of stronger enforcement, better project monitoring, increased accountability and enhanced protection for homebuyers.

What is RERA 2.0?

RERA 2.0 is a term commonly used to describe the next generation of regulatory improvements being adopted across India's real estate ecosystem.

The focus is on improving project transparency, financial accountability, complaint resolution and consumer protection.

Major Improvements Under RERA 2.0

Stronger Escrow Monitoring

Better tracking of project funds helps reduce misuse of buyer money and improves financial discipline among developers.

Better Construction Tracking

Homebuyers gain greater visibility into project progress, approvals and construction milestones.

Faster Complaint Resolution

Digital complaint filing and online monitoring improve access to dispute resolution mechanisms.

Improved Builder Accountability

Developers face greater scrutiny regarding delivery timelines, disclosures and financial management.

Benefits for Homebuyers

  • Greater project transparency
  • Improved builder accountability
  • Enhanced financial safeguards
  • Better access to project information
  • Reduced risk of project delays
  • Stronger consumer protection

Important Reminder

Even with RERA protections, buyers should independently verify project approvals, builder credentials, payment schedules and agreement terms before investing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is RERA 2.0 a new law?

No. It is an enhanced implementation and enforcement framework built on the existing RERA Act.

Can builders sell without RERA registration?

Eligible projects generally require RERA registration before marketing and sale.

Does RERA 2.0 guarantee possession on time?

No law can guarantee delivery, but stronger accountability mechanisms reduce risks.

Can buyers file complaints online?

Many state RERA authorities provide online complaint filing and status tracking.

Does RERA cover villas and apartments?

Applicable residential projects including apartments and villas may fall under RERA regulations.

Should I buy only RERA-registered projects?

Verifying RERA registration should be one of the first checks before investing.
