What are Hidden Builder Charges?

Hidden builder charges are additional costs that may not be clearly communicated during initial property discussions.

These charges often increase the final property acquisition cost significantly beyond the base price.

Common Hidden Costs

  • Clubhouse charges
  • Infrastructure development fees
  • Floor rise charges
  • Power backup fees
  • Legal documentation charges
  • PLC (Preferential Location Charges)

Parking & Maintenance Charges

Parking allocation charges and advance maintenance deposits are commonly added near possession.

Buyers should ensure these charges are clearly mentioned in the agreement.

GST & Registration Costs

GST, stamp duty and registration charges can substantially increase total acquisition cost.

These costs vary by state and property type.
