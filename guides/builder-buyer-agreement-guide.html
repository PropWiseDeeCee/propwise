Builder Buyer Agreement Guide for Indian Homebuyers

A builder buyer agreement is one of the most important legal documents in a real estate purchase. Many homebuyers sign agreements without fully understanding clauses related to possession timelines, penalties, parking rights, GST, maintenance charges, and builder liabilities.

This guide explains the key clauses every buyer should review before signing a builder agreement in India.

What is a Builder Buyer Agreement?

A builder buyer agreement is a legally binding contract between the property developer and the purchaser. It defines payment schedules, possession timelines, specifications, penalties, legal obligations, and dispute resolution mechanisms.

The agreement is usually issued after booking confirmation and before property registration.

Important Clauses You Must Check

1. Possession Timeline

The agreement should clearly specify the possession date and any grace period allowed to the builder.

Avoid agreements with vague phrases like: "reasonable extension" or "subject to approvals".

2. Delay Penalty Clause

Many agreements heavily penalize buyers for late payments while offering minimal compensation if the builder delays possession.

Ensure compensation terms are fair and clearly defined.

3. Parking Rights

Check whether parking is:

  • Covered parking or open parking
  • Included in base price or separately charged
  • Legally transferable during resale

4. Maintenance Charges

Agreements should clearly explain:

  • Monthly maintenance calculation
  • Corpus fund requirements
  • Clubhouse charges
  • Escalation clauses

5. GST and Hidden Charges

Many buyers underestimate final acquisition costs due to:

  • GST
  • Legal fees
  • Club membership charges
  • Power backup charges
  • Floor rise charges

Common Red Flags

  • One-sided builder rights
  • No delay compensation
  • Unclear possession date
  • Force majeure misuse
  • Undefined super built-up area changes
  • Mandatory maintenance lock-ins

Should You Get Legal Review?

Yes. Even reputed builders may include clauses that heavily favor the developer. A professional legal review can identify:

  • Risky clauses
  • Missing protections
  • Financial exposure
  • Registration inconsistencies

Frequently Asked Questions

Is builder buyer agreement mandatory?

Yes. It legally governs the transaction between the builder and the purchaser.

Can builder change possession date?

Only under conditions specified in the agreement. Excessive delays may trigger compensation rights under RERA.

Should I verify RERA registration?

Absolutely. Always verify project registration details on your state's RERA portal.

Can parking be charged separately?

Yes, depending on the project structure and local regulations.
