Hidden Charges While Buying Property in India

Most buyers focus only on the base price, but hidden costs can increase total cost by 10–20%.

Common Hidden Charges

  • GST (5% for under construction)
  • Legal fees
  • Maintenance deposit
  • Parking charges
  • Clubhouse charges

Why it matters

Ignoring these costs can lead to budget overruns and loan miscalculations.
