Stamp Duty & Registration Charges in Karnataka

Stamp duty, registration charges, and applicable cess typically range from about 5.5–7.6% in Karnataka depending on property value and location.

Breakdown

  • Stamp Duty: 2–5% based on property value slab
  • Registration Fee: ~2%
  • Cess/surcharge may apply depending on the property and location

These charges can change, so buyers should verify the exact payable amount on Kaveri Online Services before registration.
