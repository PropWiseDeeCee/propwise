What is Stamp Duty?

Stamp duty is a government tax paid during property registration transactions.

The amount payable depends on the property value, location and applicable state regulations.

Registration Fees

Buyers must also pay registration fees during execution of the sale deed.

  • Stamp duty
  • Registration fee
  • Applicable cess

Additional Acquisition Costs

  • Legal verification fees
  • Documentation charges
  • Loan processing fees
  • Maintenance deposits
  • Parking charges

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Karnataka stamp duty vary by property value?

Yes. Applicable rates may vary depending on the property value and category.

Are registration charges refundable?

Registration-related payments are generally non-refundable after execution.
