Base Property Price

The quoted property price is usually not the final acquisition cost. Builders often advertise only the base sale value excluding additional expenses.

Buyers should always request a complete cost sheet before making booking payments.

Registration & Stamp Duty

In Karnataka, registration and stamp duty charges may add roughly 5.5% to 7.6% depending on property value.

  • Stamp duty
  • Registration fees
  • Applicable cess

GST Charges

GST is generally applicable on under-construction properties but not on completed ready-to-move units.

Buyers should verify GST applicability directly from the builder and agreement documents.

Common Hidden Charges

  • Clubhouse charges
  • Floor rise charges
  • Legal documentation fees
  • Power backup charges
  • Infrastructure development fees
  • Corpus fund deposits

Maintenance & Parking Costs

Builders may collect advance maintenance for one or two years during possession.

Parking allocation and parking charges should be clearly documented in the agreement.

Example Cost Breakdown

  • Base Price: ₹80,00,000
  • Registration & Stamp Duty: ₹5,50,000+
  • GST: ₹4,00,000
  • Maintenance & Parking: ₹3,00,000
  • Other Charges: ₹1,50,000

Final acquisition cost may exceed the original advertised property price significantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is GST applicable on ready-to-move flats?

GST is generally not applicable on completed ready-to-move properties with completion certificate.

Are maintenance charges negotiable?

Certain maintenance and builder charges may be negotiable depending on project stage.
