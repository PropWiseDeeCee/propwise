Total Cost of Buying a Flat in Bangalore

When buying a property in Bangalore, the base price is not the final price. Buyers often underestimate additional costs like registration, GST, and maintenance.

1. Base Price

This is the property cost quoted by the builder.

2. Registration & Stamp Duty

In Karnataka, registration and stamp duty together can add around 5–7% to the property cost.

3. GST

Applicable mainly for under-construction properties (typically 5%).

4. Maintenance Charges

Builders often charge advance maintenance for 1–2 years.

5. Legal & Miscellaneous Charges

Includes legal verification, documentation, and other fees.

👉 Example Breakdown

  • Base Price: ₹50,00,000
  • Registration: ₹3,00,000
  • GST: ₹2,50,000
  • Maintenance + Other: ₹1,50,000

Total: ₹57,00,000+
