What is a Builder Buyer Agreement?

A builder buyer agreement is a legal contract between the property buyer and the builder. It defines payment schedules, possession timelines, penalties, maintenance terms, parking rights and ownership conditions.

Many buyers sign agreements without fully reviewing critical clauses, which may later lead to disputes, unexpected charges or delayed possession issues.

Important Clauses to Check

  • Possession timeline and delivery date
  • Delay compensation or penalty clause
  • GST and additional charge details
  • Parking allocation terms
  • Maintenance charges and escalation
  • Cancellation and refund policy
  • Builder liability limitations

Common Hidden Risks in Builder Agreements

Many builder agreements contain vague language such as “reasonable time” or “sole discretion” which may heavily favor the builder.

Buyers should carefully review:

  • One-sided cancellation clauses
  • Undefined possession timelines
  • Hidden maintenance escalation
  • Additional infrastructure charges
  • Parking ownership ambiguity

Frequently Asked Questions

Is builder buyer agreement legally binding?

Yes. It is a legally enforceable agreement between the buyer and builder.

Can builders change agreement terms later?

Significant changes generally require buyer consent and may also fall under RERA regulations.
