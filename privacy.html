Information We Collect

PropWise may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including your name, email address, uploaded property agreements, feedback submitted through our forms and information entered into our calculators and comparison tools.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide our services, generate AI-powered reports, improve our products, respond to support requests, maintain platform security and improve the overall user experience. We never sell your personal information to third parties.

03

Agreement Uploads

Documents uploaded to the Agreement Analyzer are used solely to generate analysis requested by the user. Uploaded documents are not shared with other users and are handled according to applicable security practices.

04

Cookies & Analytics

PropWise may use cookies, Google Analytics and similar technologies to understand website usage, improve performance, measure traffic and enhance user experience. You may disable cookies through your browser settings, although some features may not function correctly.

05

Authentication

If you create a PropWise account, authentication is securely managed using Supabase. Passwords are never stored directly by PropWise. Passwords are securely handled by our authentication provider and are never accessible to PropWise administrators.

06

We implement reasonable technical, administrative and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse or disclosure. While we strive to protect your data, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

These services maintain their own privacy policies and data handling practices. We encourage users to review those policies independently.

08

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and improve platform functionality. Users may request deletion of their account and associated data, subject to applicable legal requirements.

PropWise is intended for users who are legally capable of entering into property transactions. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. If such information is discovered, reasonable steps will be taken to delete it promptly.
